01 / MY STORY

My Story

Every person has a story. This is a little space where I share mine. Today I am going to share the things here that I used to think about a thousand times before speaking, and I could never say them.

A story where no one knows where it will take a turn. I don't think there are as many turning points in the world as I have seen in my life.

So, let's start with my family.

I belong to a family where girls are considered good only when they stay behind a veil. But I had dreams of doing something since childhood. I wanted to try everything, whether it was good or bad. But because of my family and a lack of opportunities, I couldn't do so.

I was a girl who just wanted to leave her hometown because I knew that if I stayed there, my desires would never be fulfilled.

Since childhood, I made my studies my strength because I knew that studying was the only thing that could help me reach my desires.

I was a topper during my school days. And if I talk about friendships, people in school stayed with me only because I was intelligent. I really didn't like this. The same thing happened in college too.

After college came a turning point in my life, and this turning point turned out to be something I am very grateful for.

After my final-year exams, I was thinking about what I would do next. Would I also get married after this? And what about all the hard work I had done since childhood? What would happen to all of that?

And so many negative thoughts were running through my mind.

Then I got selected for Navgurukul.

Talking to my family about coming here was not easy for me, and that day, I stood up for myself. I made myself a priority.

After coming here, I got to know myself and discovered who I actually am and what I am capable of doing.

Even before coming to Navgurukul, I had so many questions. But my questions were never given priority before. Here, not only were my questions heard, but I was also given answers. I was also taught how to find answers on my own.

Before coming here, enjoyment had become just a word for me. But now, I am enjoying my learning.

Currently, I am learning Python programming, and I have a very good relationship with Python.

Every day, I am improving myself and learning new things here.

02 / WHAT LIFE TAUGHT ME

Lessons Life Taught Me

Life has taught me to stand up for myself, make myself a priority, and never give up on my dreams just because the journey is difficult. It has taught me that education can open doors to new opportunities and bring me closer to my goals.

I have learned that my circumstances do not define my capabilities, and that I am capable of achieving more than my environment might make me believe. Life has also taught me that it's okay to question things and seek answers for myself.

I have realized that growth begins when I step outside my comfort zone and face new challenges. Most importantly, I have learned that learning doesn't have to be a burden; it can be an enjoyable journey that helps me grow and discover myself.

03 / ALWAYS LEARNING

Things I Want to Learn

I want to learn as many things as I can because I believe there is no limit to learning. I want to explore new skills, try new things, and keep growing as a person every day.

04 / LOOKING AHEAD

My Dreams

My dream is to become capable enough to help people, especially young girls from backgrounds where they have limited opportunities to explore their interests and pursue their dreams.

I want to support those who are curious, eager to learn, and want to do so much in life but lack the right opportunities or guidance to make it happen.
